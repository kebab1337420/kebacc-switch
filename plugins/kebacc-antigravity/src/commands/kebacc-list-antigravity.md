---
description: The saved Antigravity accounts, fresh numbers and time to reset
allowed-tools: Bash(~/.claude-tools/kebacc-antigravity:*), Bash(~/.claude-tools/kebacc-antigravity.exe:*)
---

Run `~/.claude-tools/kebacc-antigravity`, or `~/.claude-tools/kebacc-antigravity.exe` on Windows:

```
~/.claude-tools/kebacc-antigravity list -Provider antigravity -Refresh -Countdown
```

The numbers come from the API, not from the cache, and each account shows both its 5h and its 7d window with the time until each one resets. Repeat the lines as they are; the `*` marks the account in use.
